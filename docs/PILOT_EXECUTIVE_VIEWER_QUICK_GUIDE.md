# Pilot Executive Viewer Quick Guide

## 1. Dashboard Purpose
The dashboard provides a high-level overview of case volume, bottlenecks, and completion rates during the Pilot phase.

## 2. How to Read Total Cases
- Displays the total number of cases imported into the Staging database.
- Does not reflect the entirety of production case volume.

## 3. How to Read Pending Cases
- Cases actively being worked on by Legal Officers.

## 4. How to Read Overdue Cases
- Cases past their defined SLA.
- Note: Cases with Red Numbers ("เลขแดง") are automatically excluded from overdue metrics.

## 5. How to Read Completed Cases
- Cases marked as "เสร็จสิ้น" or assigned a Red Number.

## 6. How to Interpret Workload by Legal Officer
- Displays distribution of cases. Use this to identify resource bottlenecks.

## 7. Which Metrics Are Reliable
- Total imported cases, assigned cases, and completed cases (based on staging data).

## 8. Which Metrics Require Caution
- Overdue cases: Some cases may appear overdue if Legal Officers forget to update the status to "Completed".

## 9. What Executives Should Not Infer from the Dashboard
- Do not infer official department productivity, as this is a limited Pilot environment with a subset of data.

## 10. How to Report Suspicious Numbers
- If metrics look highly irregular (e.g., 0 completed cases when many should be), contact the Pilot Coordinator.

## 11. Pilot Limitations for Management Reporting
- Data in the Pilot dashboard is for system validation only and must not be used in official organizational reports until production launch.
